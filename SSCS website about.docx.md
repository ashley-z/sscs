## **Organizational Framework**

### **Vision**

To create a safe, inclusive, and multi-generational environment where both seniors and youth can learn, connect, and thrive, ensuring a resilient and socially connected community in the Markham area.

### **Mission**

To improve the quality of life for residents—specifically seniors, youth, and newcomers—by providing bilingual educational programs, mental wellness advocacy, and social activities that bridge generational gaps and encourage active community participation.

---

## **Expanded Primary Activities & Core Pillars**

### **1\. Mental Wellness & Advocacy**

* **Senior Emotional Health:** Workshops providing coping strategies for stress, anxiety, and isolation.  
* **Youth Mental Health Support:** Dedicated programs and resources designed to help youth navigate mental health challenges and build resilience.  
* **Safety & Fraud Prevention:** Specialized seminars to reduce senior abuse and protect residents from financial fraud.

### **2\. Health, Physical Activity & Lifelong Learning**

* **Active Living:** Daily sessions, such as Tai Chi, and health education focused on managing chronic conditions.  
* **Digital Literacy:** Training for seniors on mobile devices and platforms like Zoom to ensure those with mobility issues remain connected.  
* **Skill Development:** Offering professional arts instruction, such as our seniors' choir, to encourage new skill acquisition.

### **3\. Community Involvement & Social Integration**

* **Intergenerational Connection:** Projects that bring youth and seniors together to share experiences, reducing isolation for both groups.  
* **Cultural Celebrations:** Hosting events for Canadian and Chinese traditions (e.g., Canada Day, Lunar New Year) to strengthen community bonds.  
* **Volunteer Leadership:** Training seniors and youth to take on meaningful volunteer roles, empowering them to lead and contribute to the organization's growth.  
* **Social Outings:** Facilitating field trips to local parks and outdoor destinations to promote physical and social well-being.

### **4\. Advocacy & Outreach**

* **Bilingual Accessibility:** Delivering all programs in both English and Chinese to ensure newcomers and language-minority communities feel included.  
* **Resource Navigation:** Helping community members identify and access government grants and local resources.

---

## **Target Groups**

Our activities are strategically aligned to support:

* **Seniors & Youth** (Intergenerational Focus).  
* **Newcomers & Language Minorities**.  
* **Low-Income Families**.
